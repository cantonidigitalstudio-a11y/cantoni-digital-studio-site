        Every request should leave one clear trail for the guest and the reservations team.

        The booking flow only works if confirmation, follow-up and payment expectations remain obvious before the guest pays.

        Guest recap The buyer should see route, vehicle, extras, timing and contact details in one clean summary.

        That same recap becomes the email, WhatsApp message and payment context instead of being rebuilt manually.

        Reservations handoff The team should receive the same booking detail, with lead name, country, date, pickup point and request notes intact.

        That keeps the booking record readable even before the full payment stack is live.

        Deposit and cancellation Deposits should secure the slot, planning time and sourced extras before the team blocks inventory or staff.

        Custom styling, celebration sourcing and approved special purchases can be positioned as non-refundable once confirmed.

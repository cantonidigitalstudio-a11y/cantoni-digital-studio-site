Booking questions

Short answers reduce hesitation faster than another long luxury paragraph.

These are the questions guests usually ask before they commit to airport transfer, concierge extras or a premium arrival plan.

Do you handle airport transfers only, or also villas, resorts and chauffeur requests?

Airport pickups are the starting point, but the service can also cover villa arrivals, resort transfers, hourly chauffeur blocks and selected private experiences.

Can guests add champagne, flowers or celebration setup before arrival?

Yes. Premium add-ons, sourced celebration items and host coordination can be attached to the request before confirmation, then staged with the operations brief.

When is a booking actually confirmed?

Confirmation should happen only after the team reviews the request, availability is checked and the required deposit or payment step is completed.

What if the flight time or arrival schedule changes?

Schedule changes can still be coordinated, but they should be communicated as early as possible because vehicle allocation, driver timing and sourced extras depend on the final window.

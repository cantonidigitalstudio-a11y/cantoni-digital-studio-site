# Personalized Quote Playbook

## Objective
Generate quotes that read as a serious commercial document for one specific business, never as mass outreach.

## Brand standard
- Brand: Cantoni Digital Studio
- Logo: `assets/logo/cantoni_primary_horizontal_small.svg`
- Contact email: `cantonidigitalstudio@gmail.com`
- Tone: direct, professional, commercial
- Channel: email first, no mandatory calls

## Mandatory structure
1. Branded header
- logo
- studio name
- quote date
- client company
- website analyzed

2. Executive summary
- one paragraph on what the business does
- one paragraph on what is limiting growth right now

3. Issues found
- 2 to 4 specific findings from the real website

4. Proposed intervention
- pages / funnel
- conversion improvements
- trust and proof
- mobile / speed
- SEO if commercially relevant

5. Expected business impact
- realistic ranges
- expressed in commercial terms, not technical vanity metrics

6. Packages
- Base
- Growth (recommended)
- Optional monthly management

7. Commercial terms
- included
- excluded
- revisions
- timing
- payment schedule

## Global rule
- Quote language must match the client market language.
- Quote currency must match the client market currency.
- Internal EUR reference can be added only as a secondary internal note.

## Non-negotiable rigor rules
- A quote must be based on the current live website, not on stale CRM assumptions alone.
- Every issue listed in the quote must be traceable to a real, visible problem on the live site.
- Generic lines such as "the site could convert better" are not acceptable unless followed by the exact page or section problem.
- The recommended price must be justified by real scope, not only by a default package tier.
- If a quote was already sent to the client, do not send a revised version unless there is a clear commercial reason or an explicit client request.
- Internal previews go only to internal mailboxes. Clients do not receive draft iterations.

## Mandatory pre-send gate
Before any quote is sent, confirm all of the following:
1. Live site reviewed manually
2. At least 3 concrete site-specific issues captured
3. Solutions mapped to real pages, sections or funnel steps
4. Market served verified, not just legal city
5. Language and currency verified
6. No local paths, placeholders or technical leakage in output
7. Closing asks for decision, not for another exploratory loop

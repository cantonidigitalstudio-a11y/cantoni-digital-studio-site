# Master Templates (Language-Neutral Structure)

## 1) First outreach
- Subject: Specific website improvement in {business_name}
- Body blocks:
  - Personal opening
  - 3 concrete website issues
  - proposed improvement direction
  - single CTA: "Reply YES for audit"

## 2) Follow-up day 3
- Short reminder
- Mention value of mini roadmap
- CTA single action

## 3) Follow-up day 7
- Close thread respectfully
- Keep door open

## 4) Interested lead reply
- Ask for: URL, goal, priority service, budget range
- Confirm quote delivery within 24h

## 5) Quote structure
- project summary
- detected issues
- intervention scope
- pricing plan + optional ongoing management
- timeline
- payment terms

## 6) New service add-on reply
- confirm request
- separate module scope and fee
- impact on timeline

## Localization rule
Do not alter business logic while translating.
Only adapt language, cultural tone, and currency notation.

# Quote Packaging Profiles

## Small business
- Price logic: fast-entry offer, simple structure
- Best for: bakery, local beauty, small clinic, small service business
- Recommended range: website entry from 1200 EUR + optional monthly support
- Focus: more requests, easier contact path, stronger mobile conversion

## Local growth
- Best for: dental, clinic, beauty, legal local firms
- Focus: trust, conversion path, WhatsApp/contact, local SEO pages
- Quote style: direct commercial document with one recommended package

## International service
- Best for: concierge, hospitality, real estate, premium service businesses
- Focus: premium positioning, multilingual clarity, lead capture, trust layer
- Quote style: revenue and inquiry quality oriented

## Premium / high-ticket
- Best for: law, private healthcare, luxury, high-value advisory
- Focus: authority, proof, high-intent funnel, premium perception
- Quote style: stronger strategic framing, higher price confidence, optional monthly optimization

## Mandatory quote blocks
- business summary
- concrete issues
- proposed intervention
- expected business impact
- recommended package
- timing
- optional ongoing management

## Public baseline ladder
- Website / redesign: from 1200 EUR
- E-commerce with payments: from 4000 EUR
- Web app / platform: from 6500 EUR when scope is internal-web only, from 9000 EUR when sold as app/platform path
- Mobile or cross-platform app: from 9000 EUR
- Monthly ecosystem support: 250 / 400 / 700 EUR
- The final price must still be justified by live-site evidence, scope, integrations, urgency and materials readiness.

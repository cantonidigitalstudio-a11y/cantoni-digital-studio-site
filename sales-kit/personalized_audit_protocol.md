# Personalized Audit Protocol

## Non-negotiable rule
No email and no quote can be sent before a real audit of the target business.

## Minimum audit blocks
1. Business reality
- business name
- city / country
- sector
- current website
- primary commercial objective inferred from the site

2. Conversion issues observed
- 2 to 4 concrete problems only
- every problem must be visible on the real site
- no generic filler like "modernize the design"

3. Revenue opportunity
- explain impact in simple business language
- examples: more quote requests, more bookings, less lead loss, stronger premium perception
- use realistic ranges, never inflated claims

4. Recommended intervention
- CTA path
- landing/service architecture
- trust layer
- mobile speed / UX
- SEO intent where relevant

## Mandatory output before writing
- opening_line
- issue_1
- issue_2
- issue_3_optional
- solution_1
- solution_2
- expected_business_impact
- recommended_package
- delivery_timeline

## Quality gate
- If the same text could be sent to another company without changes, it fails.
- If the observed issues are not visible on the live site, it fails.
- If language and currency do not match the lead market, it fails.
- If the client cannot understand the economic benefit in 15 seconds, it fails.
